
I am playing arasan and stockfish againts SHumi using cutechess now. My observations so far. 
  1. Stockfish crippled to 2300 plays about even with Shumi. Arsan crippled to 2200 plays even with Shumi.
  2. When SHumi loses to better engines, the signature of the loss is always the same: "lowered pices". By 
    "lowered pieces I mean this, the material is about even, but also asymetric, ALWAYS in the fashion that
     Shumi has the second part of the below asymetries:
       a. minor piece .vs. 2-3 pawns
       b. 2 minor pieces .vs. 5-6 pawns
       c. rook .vs. 4-5 pawns. 
       d. queen .vs. rook and 3-4 pawns
       e. queen .vs. rook and minor piece and maybe a pawn.
    Shumi when losing, is always on the second side of the .vs. above. Shumi is never behind a pawn, even when losing.
